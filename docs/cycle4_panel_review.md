**Overall.** This is a careful, honest, mostly correct report with unusually good claim-level status tagging, and its arithmetic and proofs hold up. Its weaknesses are presentational and about scope discipline. (i) The text layer garbles exactly the constants the reader needs. (ii) The Lean section claims slightly more than the Lean files state. (iii) The central contrast the problem asks for, "formally verified vs headline", is argued from secondary reading and omits part of what the OpenAI package proves in Lean. (iv) Roughly 40% of the pages are secondary workstream-B and workstream-C results, which dilute the three-question story and import "reported, not re-run" claims. Fixing these would raise Clarity and Correctness by one to two points each.
